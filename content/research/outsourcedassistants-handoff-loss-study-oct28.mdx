## Question and scope

How much context survives a shift or time-zone handoff? The scope is the work record and the events the team can observe in a defined window. It does not estimate an industry rate or a legal conclusion.

## Population and unit

Tasks crossing a handoff in a defined quarter, sampled by queue. The unit is one request, handoff, assistant, or invoice. Linked events are recorded separately so a correction is not counted as an independent success.

## Definitions and measures

Repeat questions, missing owner, reopened items, and time to resume. Define the window and evidence standard before the sample begins. A count without its denominator and source is not a measure.

## Where the record fails

Handoffs fail when the note names a status but not the next action. A failure needs a reason code, a next owner, and a recheck date. Silence is not a disposition.

## Worked example

A handoff is complete when the next owner can act without calling the customer. The numbers are illustrative. The value is the stage split, which shows whether the delay belongs to intake, review, the destination, or the client.

## Evidence and limits

Public guidance can frame the questions but cannot prove a local result. Samples may exclude untracked work, offline notes, or records that never reached the client system. Record the sample frame, extraction time, and fields that were unavailable.

## Practical use

Change one controlled element at a time: the intake field, the owner, the review cadence, or the stop rule. Recheck the same measure after two cycles.
